Common questions

Is any of this real money?

No. The practice table uses play money only. There are no deposits, no withdrawals and no links to any casino or betting site. The only purchase in the app is the optional Pro subscription, which is sold through the App Store.

Does Spingalo tell me what to bet?

No. It explains how each betting system works and shows you, with thousands of simulated nights, how they tend to end. The system coach on the practice table only tells you what a chosen plan would stake next so you can see the plan in action. No system beats the house edge, and the app will not pretend otherwise.

How do I cancel Spingalo Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, choose Spingalo and tap Cancel Subscription. If you cancel during the free trial you will not be charged. If you cancel later, Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I paid for Pro but the app still shows the free version

Open the Tools tab, scroll to the Pro card and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. If that does not fix it, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the Spingalo charge. We cannot issue refunds directly, but we are happy to help you make the request.

How do Betting Buddies work?

Open the Buddies tab and share your table code or the invite link. Friends who enter the code join your table and can chat with you and swap betting plans. No account is needed. Messages sent at a table are stored on the Spingalo server so everyone at the table can read them; see the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Your lessons, sessions, logged spins and badges live only on your phone. Deleting the app removes all of it, and there is also a reset in the app that wipes it in place. If you have used Betting Buddies and want your table's messages removed from the server, email us with your table code and we will delete them.

The wheel or the table looks wrong on my phone

Spingalo supports iPhones running a recent version of iOS in portrait. Try closing the app fully and reopening it. If it still looks wrong, send us a screenshot and your iPhone model.
