What the app does

Three parts, in the order most people use them.

Learn

Seven short lessons

What roulette actually is, how to read the table, how payouts work, what the casino keeps and how to play safely. Each lesson ends with a quick check so you know it has stuck. About fifteen minutes in total.

Practise

A table with play money

An animated wheel, £100 of pretend chips and every bet a real table takes. Switch on the system coach and it tells you what Martingale, Fibonacci or d'Alembert would have you stake next, and why. Every session ends with a summary.

Understand

The Systems Lab

Pick a betting plan and run it across thousands of simulated nights. See how often it hits a target, how often it goes bust and what a typical night looks like. The numbers do the talking.
