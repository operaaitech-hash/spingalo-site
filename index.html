Three things nobody tells a new player

The wheel is simple.
The stories around it are not.

Most people learn roulette from a mate, a forum or a video selling a system. Spingalo starts from what is actually true.

The zero

One green pocket pays for the whole casino

Thirty-seven pockets, paid out as if there were thirty-six. That gap is the house edge: about £2.70 kept from every £100 bet, over time. Every lesson in Spingalo starts from that number.

Systems

A system changes the ride, not the price

Martingale, Fibonacci, Labouchere and the rest decide how much you bet next. They give you lots of small wins and a rare big loss, or the other way round. None of them changes the 2.70%.

Real edges

The only real edges are physical

A worn wheel, a tilted table, a ball you can time. Those have beaten roulette in the real world, and the app explains each one. An app that promises to predict a random spin cannot, and Spingalo says so.

Spingalo is not a tipping service. It is the friend who knows the game, sitting next to you.
