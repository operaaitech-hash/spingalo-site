Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Spingalo is an iPhone app that teaches roulette and lets you practise with play money. It is published by Opera AI Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16422655) with its registered office at Old Engineering Works, 47 Queens Road, Weybridge, KT13 9UH. This page explains what the app stores, where, and what it never does.

The short version: your progress stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is what you choose to share in Betting Buddies. There is no tracking, no advertising and no account to create.

What is stored on your device

Everything that makes the app yours is kept on your iPhone only:

  • Which lessons you have finished and your quiz scores
  • Your play-money balance, practice sessions, streak, badges and daily challenges
  • Spins you log in the bias tracker and the name you give the wheel
  • Systems Lab runs and which entries you have read in the Edges library
  • Your table code for Betting Buddies and whether Pro is active on this device

None of this is sent to us. If you delete the app, it is gone. The app also has a reset option that wipes it in place.

Betting Buddies

Betting Buddies lets friends join your table with a six-character code and chat. When you use it, the messages you send, the betting plans you share and your table code are stored on the Spingalo server so the people at your table can see them. Nothing else from the app goes with them. You do not need an account, and you can choose any name you like. If you never open Betting Buddies, nothing is sent at all.

The server is hosted for us by Render in Frankfurt, Germany. Render processes this data only to run the service, under contract and with the same protection described here.

How long it is kept. Messages, shared plans and table codes stay on the server until you ask us to remove them. To have your table's messages removed, email us with your table code (see below). We delete them within one month of your request, as UK data protection law requires.

Payments

Spingalo Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and the free trial. We never see your card details, your name or your Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy covers what it collects when you buy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking of how you use the app
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No links to gambling operators and no real-money play of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access

Children

Spingalo is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

Your rights

Because your progress lives on your phone, you already have it and can delete it whenever you like. For anything stored on the Spingalo server through Betting Buddies, you can ask us to delete it and we will. UK and EU residents have the usual rights under data protection law to access, correct and erase their data; email us and we will help. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it and the new version will be here.
